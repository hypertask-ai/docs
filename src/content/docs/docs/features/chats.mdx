Chat with agents and coworkers directly on tasks and boards.

## Chat panel

Open AI Chat from any Task detail or Board view by clicking the Chat icon on the panel bar or using the **Cmd+I** shortcut. The chat auto-opens at the task level for context-specific conversations.

- **Panel placement**: Appears at the bottom or edge of the main panel depending on viewport.
- **Auto-focus**: Resumes your last draft when you return to the panel.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**:
  - <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> — Focus Chat panel
  - <kbd>Esc</kbd> — Fold the chat panel
- Mobile: The panel overlays content; it collapses when you tap away.

## Use cases and workflows

### Co-worker chat and coordination

Chat with teammates directly on a task to plan execution, clarify requirements, or gather feedback before agents proceed. Messages create a conversation thread attached to the task, keeping context visible without leaving AI Chat.

### Agent handoffs

Assign or re-assign tasks from AI Chat by mentioning an agent. The agent receives the assignment with its assigned tasks populated, and the human’s context appears in the agent’s first reply.

### Board-wide questions

In Board views, AI Chat operates at the board scope. You can ask for summaries, status, or analytics without opening tasks, or combine board-level context with task-level replies.

## Content suggestions

AI Chat surfaces intelligent suggestions relevant to the current task or board:

- **Task context**: Summarize the task, identify dependencies, suggest subtasks, or rewrite descriptions.
- **Board context**: Ask for recent activity, pending work, or assignee clusters.
- **Won’t-help** (Ctrl/Cmd\+D): Declare suggestions irrelevant so suggestions improve for you over time.
- **Keep in thread**: Pull external content (e.g., pages, links) into the thread without dismissing the suggestion card.

## Paired tasks and concurrency

AI Chat maintains awareness of other open panels based on cross-panel awareness:

- You can open another task and use the Agent Team documentation for cross-panel handoffs.
- This keeps your conversation context between tasks in the same session.

## AI-chat gating and permissions

Chat stays visible to authorized members only. Conversation content respects per-task and per-board visibility:

- You cannot uncover private tasks or boards through AI Chat.
- Any attempt to access beyond your permissions is denied.
