# Payout cards for boards

Payout cards present payout summaries and statuses directly on board card surfaces. These surfaces integrate with pulser denominations and payout pipelines to surface transaction state in real time.

## Payout info on card surfaces

When viewing a card for a sale:
- Show payout summary details:
  - Payment currencies, amounts, and timing.
  - Payout status label (e.g., successful, pending, failed).
  - User’s applicable commissions or bonuses.
- For logged-in users, display payout identity (your share / partner share) if appropriate.
- For partner deals, surface the partner payout portion and payout tracking reference.

Payout layouts are card-level UI components tailored to the board section context (e.g., Pulsar, partner storefronts, merch trading). Use consistent symbols and order across sections to aid scanning.

## Seeing payout status

Payout status on cards:
- **Unsettled**: Card shows "Pending" or similar status until the payment network confirms.
- **Settled**: Card updates to "Paid" with a checkmark and summary.
- **Overridden or failed**: Status reads "Failed" or "Overridden" where manual or system intervention applies.

Cashtags and links permit deep dives into payout pipelines from the card surface itself.

## Design notes

- Payout card styles are a subset of the pulser-card design; they reflect the same gated-DOS language and color conventions.
- Layout adapts to limited space on smaller sections; show key metrics first with amounts or icons as fallback.

For more on payout behavior and denominations, see the [Pulser docs](/features/users/pulsers/) and [Market docs](/features/boards/marketing/).
