## October 22, 2026

### New Features

- New: Click the hints below the textbox in the desktop Ctrl+J New Task window: Attach opens the file picker, Search opens search, and Board opens the board chooser. Your text stays in the box. ([HTPR-7307](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7307))

### Improvements

- Improved: Enable **Keep AI chat closed always** in **Settings > General > Task Page** to stop docked AI chat opening automatically on tickets, boards, reloads, and after Ctrl+J task creation. You can still open chat manually, and find the setting by name in the command palette. ([HTPR-7530](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7530))

- Improved: On a ticket page, choose **Current ticket** in the docked AI chat's scope menu to focus on that ticket's title, description, and comments. The scope stays on **Current board** until you choose it and returns to **Current board** when you leave the ticket. Full-screen chat is unchanged. ([HTPR-7316](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7316))

- Improved: Slack-style search operators now autocomplete with consistent colours. Use `priority:` (urgent, high, medium, low) and `type:` (ticket, comment), or follow the search tips for `priority:high`, `type:ticket`, `"exact phrase"`, and `-word`. Quoted phrases and word exclusions stay plain text. ([HTPR-7387](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7387))

- Improved: Shortcut hints below the textbox in the desktop Ctrl+J New Task window now fit on one line. The "Esc Close" hint has been removed; Esc still closes the window. ([HTPR-7306](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7306))

## October 21, 2026

### Improvements

- Improved: After Ctrl+J creates a ticket, the AI chat sidebar shows 2 to 4 questions and improvement ideas written for that ticket. Click one to send it in the same chat. ([HTPR-7317](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7317))

## October 20, 2026

### Improvements

- Improved: The AI ticket writer follows the board's saved custom instructions in Ctrl+J compose, AI chat, and CLI/MCP. It keeps the section layout shared by past tickets, keeps sections short, and fills Number, Select, Text, Date, and Checkbox custom fields when instructions or past tickets show them. Values that do not fit the field are skipped. ([HTPR-7471](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7471))
