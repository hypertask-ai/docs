### Improvements

- Improved: The AI ticket writer follows the board's saved custom instructions in Ctrl+J compose, AI chat, and CLI/MCP. Values that do not fit the field are skipped. ([HTPR-7471](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7471))
