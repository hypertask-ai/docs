## Getting stuck at the login page

If the page appears stuck or doesn't finish loading, check these conditions:

- **Browser cookies are blocked**: Clear cookies or open the site in an incognito window to test if the issue resolves
- **Popup blockers are enabled**: Allow popups for `hypertask.ai` to ensure authentication flows complete
- **Network proxy or VPN**: Temporarily disable your proxy and try again
- **Cached credentials in a browser**: Use a different browser or clear saved credentials

## "Email not found" or "Invalid email"

Create a free account or contact your team administrator if you don't see an option to sign up.

## Resetting password

1. Click **Log in**
2. Select **Forgot password**
3. Enter your email address and follow the password-reset link sent to your inbox
4. If you don't receive a reset email, check your spam folder or contact support

## Account switching behavior

When you start a new login for a different account from the same browser:

- The previous user's session is properly cleared (Max-Age=0 cookies)
- Both Better Auth and user identity cookies are set for the new account
- You're immediately redirected to a board as the new user

Your logged-in state persists across sessions; hiding or closing the browser doesn't automatically log you out unless you explicitly sign out. Authentication is verified server-side on each request via the signed `ht_session` cookie.

## Sign-in security improvements (hypertask.ai login)

- The sign-in email endpoint no longer returns verification codes or magic-link tokens to callers. This prevents callers from using the response to reset or take over an email account without direct access to the inbox.

Your credentials and login processes otherwise remain unchanged; simply use your email and password or the password reset flow as usual. If you receive password reset emails or confirmations, verify they were sent by `hypertask.ai`; invalid or forwarded reset links are not sent by Hypertask.
