## Overview

Table view gives you a grid-based way to organize and manage tasks, similar to Notion or Linear. It adapts to the columns you need and keeps your layout preferences saved across sessions.

## Adaptive layout

Table view automatically adjusts to the columns you're using:

- **Full titles**: Task titles now take up all available width — columns you don't need are compressed or hidden, so critical information is always visible.
- **Flexible columns**: The table layout derives from your visible sections and columns, so you see the most relevant information without cramped spacing.

## Column picker

Customize your table view with the column picker:

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> and search for "Configure table" or "Column picker".
2. Select which columns to display and hide.
3. Click <strong>APPLY</strong> to persist your selection across sessions.

Your column choices are saved per user, so each team member can have their own table layout.

<Aside type="tip">
The column picker and table layout are per-user settings. You can show or hide any column without deleting it, and changes apply immediately to your view.
</Aside>

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> | Open the column picker and configure table view |

## Use cases

Table view is great for:

- **Analyzing lots of tasks**: See many items at once in a compact grid without losing readability.
- **Comparing priorities**: Side-by-side visibility of priority, size, and due dates.
- **Quick filtering**: Combine visibility filters with your column choices to surface the exact set of tasks you need.
