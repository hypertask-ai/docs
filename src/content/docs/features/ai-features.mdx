## AI-powered task creation
Let AI draft tasks for you:

### Quick add with AI
Open the **Command palette** (`Ctrl+K`) and type **"Add task"** to start a new task from anywhere in the app. You can describe what you need in plain language, and AI will generate a task with a title, description, priority, and tags.

<Aside type="tip">
Start your prompt with natural language like "Create a task to review the new backend API documentation and list TODOs."
</Aside>

### Describe, AI creates
To create a task with more control:

1. Open a board.
2. Click **Add task**.
3. In the title field, describe what you need AI to do.
4. AI automatically fills in:
   - **Title** — Clear, action-oriented task name
   - **Description** — Expanded plan or requirements
   - **Priority** — Urgent, high, medium, or low
   - **Labels** — Relevant tags for better organization

## Voice dictation
Speak your ideas directly into boards and comments. The microphone icon in the composer opens the dictation panel.

<Aside type="note">
Voice dictation is currently available on mobile devices.
</Aside>

### How it works
1. Tap the **microphone** icon in the comment composer.
2. Tap **Start** and speak your content.
3. Your speech is converted to text and inserted at the cursor position.
4. Tap **Stop** to finish. The mic is released and the dictation panel closes automatically.

### Mobile dictation fixes
On mobile iOS, voice dictation now responds correctly after using other apps:

- **Microphone release** — the green recording indicator no longer persists across app switches; dictation stops when you return to Hypertask or manually stop it
- **Dictation on send** — when you send an AI-generated message or comment, dictation stops and releases the microphone

<Aside type="tip">
Dictation uses the system's native speech-to-text. Content is processed locally where possible to protect your privacy.
</Aside>

## AI comments and replies
Let AI help you craft responses:

### AI in comments
In the comment composer, click **Ask AI** to generate a draft reply. AI drafts are grounded in the conversation history and can summarize discussions, suggest next steps, or adjust tone to match common team communication styles.

### Time command with AI
Start a comment with **`/time`** to open the time tracking panel and log hours on the current task:

1. Open a task detail page.
2. Click into the comment composer.
3. Type **`/time`** followed by your notes (optional).
4. AI opens the time panel with your description pre-filled.

## AI chat
The AI chat panel lets you query your project, generate content, and coordinate tasks without leaving the board.

<Aside type="tip">
Chat remains focused to the current board and tasks you have access to, with search for related content across all boards via the search icons.
</Aside>

### Chat scope
Choose which tasks to include in your conversation:

- **Current board** — chat conversation includes tasks and content only on the currently visible board
- **Pinned board** — chat is scoped to a specific board you've pinned in the dock
- **All boards** — show all chats and tasks across your projects

### All boards search
When in "All boards" scope, use the search icons on the left rail to find related content across boards:

- **Tasks:** search across all task titles on your visible boards
- **Chats:** find related history in AI conversations across all projects

### Previews in action alerts
Action alerts link to AI discussions with preview content showing the first sentence or key detail. This makes it quick to decide whether to open the review chat.

<Aside type="note">
Chat previews are limited to one message with a truncated body to keep notifications concise.
</Aside>

## Task enhancement
AI can help you refine tasks and descriptions:

### Polish descriptions
When editing a task description, click **Ask AI** to improve clarity, fix grammar, or restructure content. AI honors your instruction and state, then inserts the improved text at the cursor.

### Generate summaries
For long task descriptions, AI can generate a concise summary you can copy to the task title or use as a quick reference.

## Periodic task reports
The command palette includes built-in periodic reports for your tasks. Select a time range and let AI generate a readable summary:

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Task report (today)** | Review everything you worked on today |
| **Task report (this week)** | Check your priorities and progress this week |
| **Task report (this sprint)** | Historic summary of your tracked work over a sprint |

<Aside type="tip">
Reports are generated from your actual tracked time. Untracked items are not included unless you manually flag them in the pasted text.
</Aside>
