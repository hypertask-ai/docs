# Favorites

Keep your most important boards within arm's reach by pinning them to the favorites bar. Quickly jump between core workspaces without hunting through lists or filters.

## Pin Boards

1. Open any board's menu (**⌘ K** or click the board icon next to its title).
2. Toggle **Pin to favorites**. The board will appear in the favorites bar at the left rail.

## Drag to Reorder

- Grab a favorite by its drag handle (⋮⋮).
- Drag it to a new position and release.
- The sidebar reflects the new order immediately.

## Remove Favorites

- In the favorites bar, **Remove** a pinned board via the context menu (**⌘ K** or click the three dots).
- The board disappears from your favorites but remains accessible in the board list.

## System Behavior

<p>The favorites bar persists across sessions. When you reconfigure your sidebar layout, your pinned favorites stay visible under the rail. Access management and sharing settings are not affected by pinning.</p>
