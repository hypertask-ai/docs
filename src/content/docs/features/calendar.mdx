title: Calendar
description: Day, week, and month views, keyboard navigation, and shareable calendar links.
---

The calendar at `/calendar` shows every task with a due date across your boards. Filter which boards and people are included from the boards panel, and save any combination as a reusable view.

## Views

Switch views from the dropdown at the top left (or press <kbd>W</kbd> for week, <kbd>M</kbd> for month):

- **Day**: one full-width column for a single day. A week strip above it shows each day's task load as dots; click a day to jump to it, use the chevrons to move a week, or pull the handle down for a full month grid.
- **Week**: seven columns (or five with weekends hidden in calendar settings).
- **Month**: the classic grid.

Day view now has a keyboard shortcut: press <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> to switch to the day view.

Navigation works the same in every view: <kbd>U</kbd> and <kbd>I</kbd> go to the previous/next day, week, or month; <kbd>T</kbd> jumps to today; <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> move the focus day; <kbd>Shift</kbd>+arrows reschedule the focused task.

The selected day section no longer extends its background across the entire day view; only the hovered or focused row highlights. Each day row shows its own selection state.

## Shareable links

The calendar URL always encodes what you are looking at:

```
https://app.hypertask.ai/calendar?view=day&date=2026-07-31
```

- `view` is `day`, `week`, or `month`.
- `date` is the anchor day in `YYYY-MM-DD`.

The address bar updates as you navigate, so copying it always shares your current view. Opening a shared link shows that exact view and date regardless of the recipient's own last-used calendar state. Links with invalid dates fall back to the recipient's own calendar.

For agents: construct these URLs directly when pointing a person at a specific day or week, e.g. `https://app.hypertask.ai/calendar?view=week&date=2026-08-03`.
