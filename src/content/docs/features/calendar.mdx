The calendar at `/calendar` shows every task with a due date across your boards. Filter which boards and people are included from the boards panel, and save any combination as a reusable view.

## Views

Switch views from the dropdown at the top left (or press <kbd>W</kbd> for week, <kbd>M</kbd> for month):

- **Day**: full-width table layout with tasks as rows. Use <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> to switch to day view. Keyboards actions <kbd>U</kbd> and <kbd>I</kbd> move to previous/next day; <kbd>T</kbd> jumps to today; <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> move focus; <kbd>Shift</kbd>+arrows reschedule the focused task.
- **Week**: seven columns (or five with weekends hidden in calendar settings).
- **Month**: the classic grid.

## Everything split

The Everything split in the splits bar is removable and fully customizable. Change it from the default view to match how you work most often:

- **Rename**: Click the split label to edit its name. The splits row updates to show your custom title.
- **Save preferences**: Set your filters, sorting, and view type (Day, Week, Month).
- **Restore defaults**: Reset a split to its original state to undo your override.
- **Everything can never be deleted**: The split always remains available but can be hidden if you prefer.

Load a custom split with plain `/calendar` navigation. Changing the view updates the split's state; a plain load restores that custom state.

## Shareable links

The calendar URL always encodes what you are looking at:

```
https://app.hypertask.ai/calendar?view=day&date=2026-07-31
```

- `view` is `day`, `week`, or `month`.
- `date` is the anchor day in `YYYY-MM-DD`.

Copy a link at any time to share exactly the same view and date. Open a shared link to land on that day's specific view, regardless of the recipient's own last-used calendar state. Links with invalid dates fall back to the recipient's own calendar.

For agents: construct these URLs directly when pointing a person at a specific day or week, e.g. `https://app.hypertask.ai/calendar?view=week&date=2026-08-03`.
