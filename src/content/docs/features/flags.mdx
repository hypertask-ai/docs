### Flag cards with inline labels

<span class="label label--brand">Feature</span> Each flag now carries an inline label—**Bug**, **Feature**, or **Improvement**—so the intent and scope are clear at a glance.

- **Bug:** A regression or unexpected side effect.
- **Feature:** A new capability, surface, or integration.
- **Improvement:** An enhancement without preserving existing behavior.

Related flags are displayed on the card, making it easy to understand dependencies and edge cases in one view.

### Always-visible search

<span class="label label--brand">Feature</span> The search box remains on top while you scan through flags, keeping relevant information always in view without requiring re-navigation.

Use the search filter to locate flags by title, description, or label. Searches stay focused, so you can type and filter without moving your cursor.

### Reaction lifecycles on flags

<span class="label label--brand">Security</span> Flag-based triggers respect reaction lifecycles. A thumbs-up or other emoji on a flag comment never counts toward decisions after that comment is edited.

- **Before edit:** reactions on a flag comment contribute to flag-based workflow decisions
- **After edit:** reactions on that comment no longer apply; only edits to unedited comments count

This prevents an agent from reusing your earlier reaction after you refine the comment, while still letting flag owners update their reasoning without losing prior supportive reactions.
