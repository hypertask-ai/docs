The **Inbox** gathers all notifications (mentions, assignments, and more) into a single feed, letting you process them efficiently. Use keyboard shortcuts for quick actions and keep important work at the top of your queue.

## Viewing the inbox

Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> to open the command palette and search for **"Inbox"**, or go to the Inbox page from the sidebar. The inbox shows:

- **Unread notifications** from mentions, assignments, reactions, and follow updates
- **Actionable items** like lines, notifications with dismiss options (undo, dismiss all, undo all), and confirmed operations (invites, feedback)
- **Important tasks** — rows marked with the <strong>Important</strong> label appear first, in priority order and timeline-sorted (newest first)

The inbox can be grouped into three main views:

<Aside type="tip">Use <kbd>Ctrl+F</kbd> from the Inbox to search across all notifications. This is scoped across boards for board-agnostic filtering.</Aside>

### Inbox views

| View | Shortcut | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Inbox** | Default | All notifications |
| **Search** | Search icon | Text-based search across all boards |
| **Notifications** | Notification icon | List of all notification types |

## Snooze

Temporarily hide notifications from appearing in your feed. Snoozed items are resurfaced after the specified duration on a specified date.

Use **Snooze** in two ways:

1. **Notification actions** — click the S button on a notification and choose a future time
2. **Task Snooze** — within a task detail, use <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> to open the command palette and run **Snooze**, then pick the duration and date. The notification is hidden until the timer expires.

When a snoozed notification resurfaces, it re-enters your inbox at the top of the list.

## Awaiting Delete notifications

Notifications that trigger a follow-up action (such as undo or dismiss) include an **Awaiting Delete** badge and show action buttons:

- **Undo** — Revert the operation (e.g., re-create a deleted task, re-add a dismissed notification)
- **Dismiss** — Hide the notification and block it from returning (e.g., clear invites that were rejected)
- **Undo all** or **Dismiss all** — Apply the action to all matching rows in the filtered list

These notifications appear at the top of the list because they require your decision.

## Important

**Important** represents tasks and notifications that matter specifically to you, filtering the inbox to show only rows relevant to your work. Tasks in Importance qualify based on:

- Addressed to you (mentions, assignments, your own reminders, returned snoozes)
- Live context (the task itself, comments on your tasks, overdue status for tasks you're personally assigned)
- Your involvement (description edits for tasks you're personally assigned)

Rows in Important are shown in priority order and timeline-sorted (oldest first when they became Important; updated rows bubble to the top). Use the <strong>Important</strong> filter in the inbox to focus only on your most relevant activity.

<Aside type="note">Important is applied to all workspaces where you have permissions. Tasks marked Important in one board are visible in the Important view of that board.</Aside>

## Bookmarking

Mark notifications and comments with the star icon to pin them as favorites. Bookmarked items appear at the top of the list, helping you quickly access key updates or important threads.

## Fallback for no notifications

If your team hasn't sent you any notifications, the Inbox shows a friendly empty state encouraging you to enable notifications or check your alerts on the board.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> — Open command palette with inbox options (Snooze, Undo, Mark Read)
- <kbd>Ctrl+F</kbd> — Search across all notifications
- <kbd>N</kbd> — Create a new task from the active board
- <kbd>Space</kbd> — Mark current item as read/unread
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> — Close modal, draft error, or focused search
