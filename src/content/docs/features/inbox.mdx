The **Inbox** gathers all notifications (mentions, assignments, and more) into a single feed, letting you process them efficiently. Use keyboard shortcuts for quick actions and keep important work at the top of your queue.

## Viewing the inbox

Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> to open the command palette and search for **"Inbox"**, or go to the Inbox page from the sidebar. The inbox shows:

- **Unread notifications** from mentions, assignments, reactions, and follow updates
- **Actionable items** like tasks, notifications with dismiss options (undo, dismiss all, undo all), and confirmed operations (invites, feedback)
- **Important tasks** — rows marked with the <strong>Important</strong> label appear first, in priority order and timeline-sorted (newest first)

The inbox can be grouped into multiple views:

<Aside type="tip">Use <kbd>Ctrl+F</kbd> from the Inbox to search across all notifications. This is scoped across boards for board-agnostic filtering.</Aside>

### Inbox views

| View | Shortcut | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Inbox** | Default | All notifications |
| **Search** | Search icon | Text-based search across all boards |
| **Notifications** | Notification icon | List of all notification types |

## Split views and empty state

Inboxes may be split into multiple panes (e.g., "Awaiting reply" and "Mentions"). Each pane determines whether the overall inbox is empty:

- Only the **active split** considers whether any items exist.
- Setting one split to empty does not affect other splits.
- Split labels and counts are shown per pane, helping you stay oriented.

## Read receipts

Turn on read receipts in a board's Settings page to get visibility into whether others have seen your comments:

- **Toggle per board**: Go to Settings and enable read receipts for a specific board.
- Start receiving receipts once you turn them on: receipts appear as soon as other members of the board have read your comments.

Use read receipts to track whether your messages were seen without sending a follow-up.

## Mention-based notifications

When someone @mentions you on a task or comment, the notification appears in your inbox with a personal touch. Mention email notifications now:
- **Lead with your name** — The email greeting starts with "Hi [Your Name]," making it feel more direct and readable
- **Signal 1:1 mentions** — A clear badge or visual indicator marks the mention as a direct tag, distinguishing it from channel-wide tags or other notifications
- **Include personal CTAs** — Call-to-action buttons in the notification (or inbox view) are tailored to action on the mentioned task
- **Show reply preview in snippet** — When someone replies below a mention, the reply preview strips blockquotes, empty headers (like "@name said"), and other quoted content before displaying the actual message in the inbox

For moderate mention volume, tasks appear directly in your inbox with the mention context attached. For high mention frequency, the inbox aggregates mentions you can review at a glance while still preserving the one-on-one nature of individual tags.

## Snooze

Temporarily hide notifications from appearing in your feed. Snoozed items are resurfaced after the specified duration on a specified date.

Use **Snooze** in two ways:
1. **Notification actions** — click the S button on a notification and choose a future time
2. **Task Snooze** — within a task detail, use <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> to open the command palette and run **Snooze**, then pick the duration and date. The notification is hidden until the timer expires.

When a snoozed notification resurfaces, it re-enters your inbox at the top of the list.

## Agent notifications

Agent notifications include mentions directed at agents (e.g., "Reply to @dev-bot"). These are handled with special guard-rules to prevent them from being incorrectly archived during snooze or reminder lookups. The system skips reminder validation when the notification is addressed to an agent, ensuring agent rows stay visible until you explicitly take action on them.

### Real-time agent inbox

Agent notifications now update in real-time using WebSocket connections. As agent mentions, assignments, or follow-ups occur, the server broadcasts updates instantly to all subscribed clients on app, CLI, MCP, and manifest surfaces. This works in parallel with general real-time inbox updates described in the next section.

## Awaiting Delete notifications

Notifications that trigger a follow-up action (such as undo or dismiss) include an **Awaiting Delete** badge and show action buttons:

- **Undo** — Revert the operation (e.g., re-create a deleted task, re-add a dismissed notification)
- **Dismiss** — Hide the notification and block it from returning (e.g., clear invites that were rejected)
- **Undo all** or **Dismiss all** — Apply the action to all matching rows in the filtered list

These notifications appear at the top of the list because they require your decision.

## Important

**Important** represents tasks and notifications that matter specifically to you, filtering the inbox to show only rows relevant to your work. Tasks in Importance qualify based on:
- Addressed to you (mentions, assignments, your own reminders, returned snoozes)
- Live context (the task itself, comments on your tasks, overdue status for tasks you're personally assigned)
- Your involvement (description edits for tasks you're personally assigned)

Rows in Important are shown in priority order and timeline-sorted (oldest first when they became Important; updated rows bubble to the top). Use the <strong>Important</strong> filter in the inbox to focus only on your most relevant activity.

<Aside type="note">Important is applied to all workspaces where you have permissions. Tasks marked Important in one board are visible in the Important view of that board.</Aside>

## Bookmarking

Mark notifications and comments with the star icon to pin them as favorites. Bookmarked items appear at the top of the list, helping you quickly access key updates or important threads.

## Inbox layout

Starting mid-August 2026, the Inbox card layout has been refined:
- Cards now **hug the bottom** of the feed when quick tips are turned off, or rise with the quick-tips bar when enabled.
- The previously dead 36px gap under cards is gone, giving a tighter, more compact column for large notification counts.
- These changes apply globally across boards and splits but can be toggled per board via Quick Tips in the feed or command palette.

## Real-time inbox updates

Inbox notifications now update in real-time using WebSocket connections. As notifications are created or changed, the server broadcasts updates to all connected clients, eliminating manual page refreshes.

- **Subscription on client**: `useInboxRealtime` hook in the frontend layer subscribes to inbox changes for the current user.
- **Broadcast mechanism**: `broadcastInboxChange` fires whenever a notification is created or modified, notifying all subscribed clients instantly across all supported surfaces (app, CLI, MCP, manifest, AI chat).

This ensures your inbox always reflects the latest state of mentions, assignments, reactions, and follow updates without needing to refresh manually.

## Post-delete and post-archive behavior

When you delete or archive a task, the inbox updates accordingly:
- **Archive** now hides the row in the inbox (previously it lingered).
- **Delete** now removes the row live, so deleted tasks no longer appear in queries or MCP endpoints.

All reads (inbox, badge count, agent inbox, MCP) now require the task to be normal, ensuring deleted or archived items are excluded consistently.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> — Open command palette with inbox options (Snooze, Read/Unread, Mark Read)
- <kbd>Ctrl+F</kbd> — Search across all notifications
- <kbd>N</kbd> — Create a new task from the active board
- <kbd>Space</kbd> — Mark current item as read/unread
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> — Close modal, draft error, or focused search
