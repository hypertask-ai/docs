The **Inbox** gathers all notifications (mentions, assignments, and more) into a single feed, letting you process them efficiently. Use keyboard shortcuts for quick actions and keep important work at the top of your queue.

## Viewing the inbox

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> to open the command palette and search for **"Inbox"**, or go to the Inbox page from the sidebar. The inbox shows:
- **Unread notifications** from mentions, assignments, reactions, and follow updates
- **Actionable items** like tasks, notifications with dismiss options (undo, dismiss all, undo all), and confirmed operations (invites, feedback)
- **Important tasks** — rows marked with the <strong>Important</strong> label appear first, in priority order and timeline-sorted (newest first)

The inbox can be grouped into multiple views:

<Aside type="tip">Use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> from the Inbox to search across all notifications. This is scoped across boards for board-agnostic filtering.</Aside>

### Inbox views

| View | Shortcut | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Inbox** | Default | All notifications |
| **Search** | Search icon | Text-based search across all boards |
| **Notifications** | Notification icon | List of all notification types |

## Agent notifications

Agent notifications include mentions directed at agents (e.g., "Reply to @dev-bot"). These are handled with special guard-rules to prevent them from being incorrectly archived during snooze or reminder lookups. The system skips reminder validation when the notification is addressed to an agent, ensuring agent rows stay visible until you explicitly take action on them.

### Guest Hyper AI seeded notifications

Fresh guest inboxes now receive 4 seeded notifications from a Hyper AI agent. When available, the Important split is enabled, helping new users immediately experience AI-driven prioritization.

## OpenWiki

OpenWiki is a RAG-powered notepad available on each task, supporting AI-optimized queries and your custom documentation. When you add notes to a task, they're versioned and can be queried by agents whenever they investigate that task.

OpenWiki notifications include an **OpenWiki** badge and action buttons:
- **View** — Open the OpenWiki page for the associated task
- **Refresh** — Pull the latest RAG content from connected documentation
- **Dismiss** — Remove the notification from your inbox without affecting the underlying task
- **Undo** — Re-add a dismissed OpenWiki notification

To keep up-to-date with OpenWiki activity, use the Refresh button in the notification card, which fetches fresh content from your connected knowledge base.

## OpenWiki refresh

OpenWiki refresh periodically pulls the latest RAG content from connected documentation sources. If OpenWiki operations are failing, check your gateway configuration in `~/.openwiki/.env`:

```bash
# Ensure you're pointing to the Cloudflare AI Gateway
OPENWIKI_GATEWAY=https://gateway.ai.cloudflare.com/v1/YOUR_ACCOUNT/YOUR_SERVICE
```

## Split views and empty state

Inboxes may include the Important split to group tasks by importance, helping you focus on the most relevant items at the top of your feed:

### Important split

Use the <strong>Important</strong> split to keep high-priority tasks visible first:
- Shows only tasks and notifications marked as Important
- Rows appear in priority order and timeline-sorted (oldest when they became Important; updated rows bubble to the top)
- Tasks qualify as Important when they're addressed to you (mentions, assignments, your own reminders) and still active in your current board

<Aside type="info">
The Important split is automatically enabled for fresh guest inboxes and provides immediate clarity on your high-priority work.
</Aside>

## What an inbox row shows

Each row is `sender (unread count) · subject · chips · timestamp`.

- **The number in brackets beside the sender is the unread count for that ticket, not its total comments.** A ticket with 27 comments of which 2 are unread shows `(2)`. The bracket is omitted when nothing is unread.
- **The blue dot** still marks the row itself as unseen. The bracket and the dot answer different questions: the dot is "this row is new", the bracket is "this ticket has N things you have not read".
- **An overdue task carries no "Task is overdue" text.** The due-date chip renders in the overdue red instead, so the signal is the date rather than a phrase repeated on every row.

## Archived tasks take their notifications with them

**Notifications on an archived task do not appear in the inbox, including `@mentions`.** The inbox query filters to tasks with `status: Normal`.

The notification rows are still created in the database; the inbox simply does not display them. So a comment or a mention posted on a ticket you have already archived reaches nobody, whether a person or an agent posted it.

If you need someone to see a comment, check the ticket is not archived first.

## Read receipts

Turn on read receipts in a board's Settings page to get visibility into whether others have seen your comments:
- **Toggle per board:** Go to Settings and enable read receipts for a specific board.
- Start receiving receipts once you turn them on: receipts appear as soon as other members of the board have read your comments.

Use read receipts to track whether your messages were seen without sending a follow-up.

## Mention-based notifications

When someone @mentions you on a task or comment, the notification appears in your inbox with a personal touch. Mention email notifications now:
- **Lead with your name** — The email greeting starts with "Hi [Your Name]," making it feel more direct and readable
- **Signal 1:1 mentions** — A clear badge or visual indicator marks the mention as a direct tag, distinguishing it from channel-wide tags or other notifications
- **Include personal CTAs** — Call-to-action buttons in the notification (or inbox view) are tailored to action on the mentioned task
- **Show reply preview in snippet** — When someone replies below a mention, the reply preview strips blockquotes, empty headers (like "@name said"), and other quoted content before displaying the actual message in the inbox

For moderate mention volume, tasks appear directly in your inbox with the mention context attached. For high mention frequency, the inbox aggregates mentions you can review at a glance while still preserving the one-on-one nature of individual tags.

## Snooze

Temporarily hide notifications from appearing in your feed. Snoozed items are resurfaced after the specified duration on a specified date.

Use **Snooze** in two ways:
1. **Notification actions** — click the S button on a notification and choose a future time
2. **Task Snooze** — within a task detail, use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> to open the command palette and run **Snooze**, then pick the duration and date. The notification is hidden until the timer expires.

When a snoozed notification resurfaces, it re-enters your inbox at the top of the list.

### Real-time agent inbox

Agent notifications now update in real-time using WebSocket connections. As agent mentions, assignments, or follow-ups occur, the server broadcasts updates instantly to all subscribed clients on app, CLI, MCP, and manifest surfaces. This works in parallel with general real-time inbox updates described in the next section.

### Guest Hyper AI seeded notifications

Fresh guest inboxes now receive 4 seeded notifications from a Hyper AI agent. When available, the Important split is enabled, helping new users immediately experience AI-driven prioritization.

### Demo sign-in: guest boards only for new empty accounts

When you sign in via demo links, existing accounts that already own or belong to any board receive no new guest seeds. Guest inboxes on new accounts are limited to 4 seeded notifications from a Hyper AI agent, and guest rows expire automatically after 24 hours. This keeps demo environments focused on fresh adoption without clogging accounts with pre-existing content.

## User unsubscribe on notification emails

Each notification email now includes one-click unsubscribe headers that work with Gmail, Yahoo, and other major mail clients. The unsubscribe flow is handled entirely server-side through a no-auth endpoint, requiring no login to opt out of future notification emails.

If you frequently receive too many email notifications, you can unsubscribe from the list view using the action button on select notifications to permanently turn off email delivery for that category.

<Aside type="note">Unsubscribing affects all notification deliveries across boards. To adjust per-board or per-type granularity, use board settings and notification controls.</Aside>

## Awaiting Delete notifications

Notifications that trigger a follow-up action (such as undo or dismiss) include an **Awaiting Delete** badge and show action buttons:
- **Undo** — Revert the operation (e.g., re-create a deleted task, re-add a dismissed notification)
- **Dismiss** — Hide the notification and block it from returning (e.g., clear invites that were rejected)
- **Undo all** or **Dismiss all** — Apply the action to all matching rows in the filtered list

These notifications appear at the top of the list because they require your decision.

## Important

**Important** represents tasks and notifications that matter specifically to you, filtering the inbox to show only rows relevant to your work. Tasks in Importance qualify based on:
- Addressed to you (mentions, assignments, your own reminders, returned snoozes)
- Live context (the task itself, comments on your tasks, overdue status for tasks you're personally assigned)
- Your involvement (description edits for tasks you're personally assigned)

Rows in Important are shown in priority order and timeline-sorted (oldest first when they became Important; updated rows bubble to the top). Use the <strong>Important</strong> filter in the inbox to focus only on your most relevant activity.

<Aside type="note">Important is applied to all workspaces where you have permissions. Tasks marked Important in one board are visible in the Important view of that board.</Aside>

## Bookmarking

Mark notifications and comments with the star icon to pin them as favorites. Bookmarked items appear at the top of the list, helping you quickly access key updates or important threads.

## Inbox header layout

The inbox and calendar header rows now share guest CTAs (e.g., "New task" and "Add board") with calendar filter controls in a unified gutter, maintaining consistent visual alignment across surfaces. This reflow keeps both inbox and calendar headers in sync for a clearer touch target grid.

## Real-time inbox updates

Inbox notifications now update in real-time using WebSocket connections. As notifications are created or changed, the server broadcasts updates to all connected clients, eliminating manual page refreshes.

- **Subscription on client:** `useInboxRealtime` hook in the frontend layer subscribes to inbox changes for the current user.
- **Broadcast mechanism:** `broadcastInboxChange` fires whenever a notification is created or modified, notifying all subscribed clients instantly across all supported surfaces (app, CLI, MCP, manifest).

This ensures your inbox always reflects the latest state of mentions, assignments, reactions, and follow updates without needing to refresh manually.

## Post-delete and post-archive behavior

When you delete or archive a task, the inbox updates accordingly:
- **Archive** now hides the row in the inbox (previously it lingered)
- **Delete** now removes the row live, so deleted tasks no longer appear in queries or MCP endpoints

All reads (inbox, badge count, agent inbox, MCP) now require the task to be normal, ensuring deleted or archived items are excluded consistently.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> — Open command palette with inbox options (Snooze, Read/Unread, Mark Read)
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> — Search across all notifications
- <kbd>N</kbd> — Create a new task from the active board
- <kbd>Space</kbd> — Mark current item as read/unread
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> — Close modal, draft error, or focused search

### Swipe-through-unread (mobile only)

Use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> → **Swipe through unread** to open the card stack over the inbox unread list:
- Swipe right or press → to mark the notification as read
- Swipe left or press ← to keep it unread
- Undo is available at the top of the stack
- One bulk archive call runs on exit to clean up your feed

This gives you a Tinder-style review of your inbox items on mobile, with keyboard support on desktop.
