The ticket detail panel now responds smoothly to the AI sidebar opening and resizing across different window widths. In windows around **1024–1180px** with the AI sidebar fully open, ticket details render on the right side and stack under comments and assignees in a stable right-rail layout.

This resolves cases where layout shifts moved details out of view or caused partial cut-offs at intermediate widths.
