summary: "New AI chats are named after the associated ticket, the board, or your first message. A follow-up rename preserves your choice."
---

AI chats now start with a meaningful name instead of "New AI Chat". System default names are: if attached to a ticket, the chat uses the ticket title; otherwise it uses the board name. After the first message, the chat takes that message as its name. You can rename chats manually, and any rename persists for future use.

This naming behavior starts automatically: open AI Chat on any board or ticket, and the first chat you create shows the board or ticket as its title without any action from you. Once you exchange a few messages, the chat adopts the first message as its name, making it easier to identify threads later. Renames are remembered, so you can keep the naming style that matches your workflow.
