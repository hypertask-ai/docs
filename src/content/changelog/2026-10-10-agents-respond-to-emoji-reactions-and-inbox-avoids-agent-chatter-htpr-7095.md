Agents that receive comment reactions now get a thumbs-up on a comment as an immediate signal from the human. Hypertask sends the reaction to the targeted runner in seconds and the reaction persists on the task. For inbox cleanup, only questions and real @mentions now land in the inbox, while FYI agent chatter appears in the detail but not in your top row. This keeps your inbox focused on work that needs attention.
