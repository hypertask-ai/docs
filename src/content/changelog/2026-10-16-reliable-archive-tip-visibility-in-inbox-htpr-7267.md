---

When using keyboard archive shortcuts (Ctrl+E or Escape) to clear notifications and tasks in the Inbox, a tip indicating which shortcut to press to archive now appears reliably. Previously the tip could be hidden when the bottom shortcut bar was closed or after multiple use of archive shortcuts that led to inbox zero. Opening the bottom shortcut bar or navigating to the Inbox makes the tip visible again.

This fixes an issue where the tip did not show consistently on app.hypertask.ai after clearing items via archive actions.
