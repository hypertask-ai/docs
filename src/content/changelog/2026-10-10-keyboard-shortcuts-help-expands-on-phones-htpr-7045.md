tags: ["Keyboard"]
summary: "Keyboard shortcuts help on phones now fills the screen and shows all key labels fully."
---

On mobile devices, the keyboard shortcuts help panel was previously squeezed into a narrow strip that cut off key labels. The panel has been expanded to use the full screen width, ensuring that all key badges are fully visible on one or two lines. This change applies across all four light/dark themes and improves readability when viewing shortcuts on phones.
