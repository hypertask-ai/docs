---

Pressing Back after opening a subtask now shows the parent task immediately instead of showing the subtask for up to a few seconds. Once you take a Forward action, pressing Back returns to the subtask if needed. Opening a task directly from saved data shows it reliably within about 0.3 seconds on both desktop and mobile, eliminating previous delays.
