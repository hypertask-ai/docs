---

Members of a board now correctly see limited profile information for the board owner and assignees when viewing the old board detail answer. Previously, private profile fields were displayed to all members. The answer now shows each person with only their id, name, and photo.

This improves privacy by ensuring only non-sensitive fields are publicly visible on the board detail.
