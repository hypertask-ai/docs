Decisions is a new organization surface to unblock work waiting for your approval. In your inbox, a Decisions split appears next to Important to show tasks pinned to your approval, and on any board, a Decisions view surfaces all "waiting on" tickets that expect you. The split pulls from any label you clip, so it’s automatically scoped to the work that owns you as the final step.
