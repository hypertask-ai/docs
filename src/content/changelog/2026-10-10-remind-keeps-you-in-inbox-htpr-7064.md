date: "2026-10-10"
title: "Remind keeps you in Inbox"
tags: ["Inbox"]
summary: "Choosing Remind from an Inbox notification no longer opens the task and keeps the notification around."
---

When you open an Inbox notification card and choose "Remind" with a time such as "Later today" or "Tomorrow," the Remind panel now closes immediately. Your Inbox view stays open and the notification is removed without an extra click. The previous behavior would open the target task and leave the notification in the Inbox, requiring a return step. This change makes Reminders a one-click, task-agnostic action while preserving their time-aware clearing behavior.
