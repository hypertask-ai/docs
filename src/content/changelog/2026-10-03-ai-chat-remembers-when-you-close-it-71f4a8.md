Closing AI Chat keeps it closed after a reload. The panel respects your default setting and manual close action.

## Details

AI chat persistence across reload: closing the AI chat panel on boards, ticket pages, My Tasks, Inbox, and Calendar keeps it closed after a page reload. The panel now correctly respects the "Open AI chat by default" setting and your manual close action (HTPR-6835).
