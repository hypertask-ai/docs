Built-in views such as Overdue and My Tasks can be dragged anywhere in the view bar and managed like ordinary views.

## Details

Added: Built-in view tabs (Overdue, Blocked, My Tasks, Agents) are now ordinary orderable views: drag them anywhere in the bar including up to an earlier row, and they show in Manage views with drag + hide (HTPR-5011)
