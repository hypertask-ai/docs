Users can now receive more relevant suggestions in AI chat panels tied to the specific ticket context. Previously, the server sometimes returned "No output generated" instead of suggestions, leading to generic recommendations that didn't match the ticket. This updated Haiku 5.5 behavior now returns expected output correctly. AI-powered suggestions are more targeted to the ticket's subject and related information.
