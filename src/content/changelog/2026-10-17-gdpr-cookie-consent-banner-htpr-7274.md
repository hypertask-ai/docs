Every visitor now sees a cookie banner on login, signup, and when opening a signed-in board. Tracking cookies and IP lookups are not stored or performed until you select an option. Your tracking preferences on hypertask.ai are also shared across all Hypertask products, and tracked behavior follows your choice.

This ensures your data is not collected without your knowledge or agreement. The consent experience applies across login, signup, and board views so that your preferences apply consistently wherever you start a session.
