---

When you open a ticket from the Decisions split in your inbox, Hypertask now jumps to the comment that asked you the question, instead of the top of the ticket. On desktop the question is also highlighted. Clicking, tapping on phone and pressing Enter on the selected row all land in the same place. Opening a ticket from Important works as before.
