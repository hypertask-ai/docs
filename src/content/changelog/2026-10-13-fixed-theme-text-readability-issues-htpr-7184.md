Fixed a visual issue where the “404” page had white text on a white background in Graphite and AMOLED themes, making it unreadable. Users can now see the error message clearly in all theme variants: Porcelain (dark text), Graphite (white text on near-black), AMOLED (light grey on black), and Dia (high-contrast). The fix ensures consistent visibility across visual themes.
