An assigned task now retains its human assignee after closing the assign picker or pressing Escape. The assignee name remains on the card through page reloads and subsequent checks, whether assigned from the board card or from a direct task link. This change applies to all users. ([HTPR-6962](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/6962)).
