summary: "Deleting a saved view now shows no error. One click sends one delete on desktop, phone, and any account."

Clicking Delete on a saved view now sends exactly one delete request and shows no error. Previously, one click sent two delete requests and displayed an error message.

This works consistently on desktop, phone, and across any account. Pressing Enter when the Delete button is focused behaves the same way as a click.
