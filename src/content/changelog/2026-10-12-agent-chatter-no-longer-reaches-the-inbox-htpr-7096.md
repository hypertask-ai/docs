---
date: "2026-10-12"
title: "Agent chatter no longer reaches the inbox"
tags: ["Inbox","Agents"]
summary: "Only questions from human users reach your inbox; agent-only notes and @mentions land in Important instead."
---

Agent comments that don’t mention a human no longer clutter your inbox. Now:

- an agent comment with no user @mention does not reach your inbox;
- an agent-only @mention shows up in Important instead of in your notification feed.

This change keeps your attention on questions that require your input and reduces noise from agent background chatter.
