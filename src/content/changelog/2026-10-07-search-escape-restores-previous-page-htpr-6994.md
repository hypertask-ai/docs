title: "Search Escape restores previous page"
tags: ["Search"]
summary: "Press / to open search, then one Escape closes it and returns you to the exact page you came from."
---

When you open the search by pressing **/**, one single Escape now closes the search view and returns you to the exact page you were viewing. Previously, pressing Escape would leave search open in some situations or require another press.

This also applies when you typed a word and received search suggestions. A single Escape now closes both the suggestions and returns you to your previous location.
