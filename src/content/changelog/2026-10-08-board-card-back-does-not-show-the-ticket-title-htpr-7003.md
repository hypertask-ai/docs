When you open a board card and press **Back** within 0.3 seconds, the board appears immediately with no title flash. The board is visible within 1 second in at least 19 of 20 quick Back actions, eliminating the previous behavior where the ticket title briefly showed before the board. This complements the fix for Backing from subtasks.
