tags: ["Tasks"]
summary: "A shared comment link now opens on that comment instead of the ticket top"
---

When you open a link to a comment for example from Slack email or another app the page now scrolls to and focuses that comment instead of loading the ticket near the header

You can easily share comments by copying their URLs. When someone follows the link any shared comment link now opens on that comment also in a new tab making it easier to reference specific points in a conversation

This fix applies to long threads with hundreds of comments and to narrow phones where comments previously scrolled far down the page [https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7090](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7090)
