---

When you delete a task, an Undo button appears on the task page notification. Clicking **Undo** immediately restores the task to its original column without refreshing the page. The feature works for bulk deletions too, and with keyboard shortcuts Ctrl+Z or Ctrl+Y, fixing an issue where Undo gestures were lost on page reloads [https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7185](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7185).
