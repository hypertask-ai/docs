tags: ["Board"]
summary: "After closing a task with Escape, priority, size and tags now update on the card immediately."
---

Change priority, size and tag on a task, then press ``Escape`` to close it. Previously, the board card would retain the old values until the task was reopened. Now the updated fields appear right away (within 0.3–0.9 seconds in QA testing).

Fixes: HTPR-7129
