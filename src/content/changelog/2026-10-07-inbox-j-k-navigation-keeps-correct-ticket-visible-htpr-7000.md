tags: ["Inbox","Keyboard"]
summary: "J and the next-arrow keys now reliably open the correct Inbox item without an accidental 15-second delay on stale content."
---

Pressing J or clicking the next-arrow several times while viewing an Inbox item no longer causes the board to display the wrong task for more than 15 seconds. Instead, the shortcut immediately opens and aligns focus with the correct notification or ticket, making sound-based navigation (e.g., scanning rows while in another application) predictable and responsive.

The previous behavior would occasionally leave the wrong ticket on screen and keep the UI in a misleading state, causing confusion when users relied on visual cues alongside keyboard movement.

This change ensures that keyboard shortcuts and IME-aware navigation behave synchronously with the visible content, allowing you to swipe through the inbox quickly without losing track of which item is active.
