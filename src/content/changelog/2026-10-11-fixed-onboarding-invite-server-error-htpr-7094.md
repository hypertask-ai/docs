The final step of the new user onboarding flow now completes without causing a server error or visible failure. Previously, users who reached the "invite teammates" step would encounter an unhelpful technical error and see no invite link at all, blocking their journey further. This change restores a smoother onboarding path that allows new teams to invite their members immediately after creating a project.
