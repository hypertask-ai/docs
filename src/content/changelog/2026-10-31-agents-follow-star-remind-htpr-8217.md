---

An agent could not keep track of a ticket the way a person does. It can now:

- Follow a ticket, so it appears in that ticket's follower list until the agent unfollows.
- Star a ticket, which bookmarks it privately for that agent. Unstar removes it.
- Set a reminder on a ticket. The ticket comes back to the agent's inbox at that time, and a later reminder replaces an earlier one that has not fired.
- Read all three states back in one call.

The same tool is available in AI chat. Board access, the agent write role and a ticket switch still apply, and a person's own app writes are unchanged.
