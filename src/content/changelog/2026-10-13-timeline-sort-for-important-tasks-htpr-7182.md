**Inbox Sorting with Timeline Order**

Task rows marked **Important** now appear first in the Inbox. They are sorted by priority and by date, with new items placed at the top. This makes it easier to identify and act on your most urgent tasks immediately.

For details, see [HTPR-7182](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7182).
