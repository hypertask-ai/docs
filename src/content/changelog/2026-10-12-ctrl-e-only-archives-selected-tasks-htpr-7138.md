Ctrl+E (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+E (Mac) now archives only the currently selected tasks instead of also archiving the next adjacent unselected tasks. With multiple tasks selected, Ctrl+E archives exactly that selection; the next ticket stays. Undo restores all archived selections together. Tested and QA passed on production.
