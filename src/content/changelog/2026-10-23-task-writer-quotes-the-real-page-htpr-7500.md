When you ask the task writer for an experiment ticket, it now starts from what is live today instead of a guess.

- The Control section quotes the real headline, subline and button text from the ticket or page you point it to.
- If that copy is missing, the writer says so instead of inventing a quote.
- Impact, Confidence and Ease each come with a one-line reason, plus a total.
- The ticket keeps its Requirements section and stays short.

Documentation: https://docs.hypertask.ai/features/ai-features/ [HTPR-7500](https://app.hypertask.ai/detail/project-15/7500)
