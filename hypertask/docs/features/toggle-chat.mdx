<Aside type="tip">
You can also open /chat or open the AI chat rail on the change list (tracked by <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+?</kbd>) and close it with the normal close buttons.
</Aside>

On the board's change list (digits <kbd>5</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+?</kbd>), there's a toggle to show/hide the docked AI chat. Click the toggle icon (eye/crossed-eye) to open or close the chat. This also affects rail behavior: when the chat is closed, the rail hides it. When open, it opens/toggles the chat panel.
## Sending in full-screen chat

Open `/chat` for the full-screen composer. It keeps one row of controls at the bottom:

- **Attach** and **@** add a file or a context item.
- **Mic** sits left of **Send**. Hovering it shows `Dictate · Ctrl Shift D`.
- **Send** is a text button. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the same result.
- The placeholder reads `Ask anything, @ for context`. Hover the box to see the `Focus · Ctrl Q` hint.

While you dictate, the mic is replaced by a pill with the elapsed time, a cancel button and a done button.
