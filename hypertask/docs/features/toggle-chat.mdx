---
title: Toggle Docked Chat
description: Toggle the docked AI chat from the board change list (rail).
---

import { Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<Aside type="tip">
You can also open /chat or open the AI chat rail on the change list (tracked by <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+?</kbd>) and close it with the normal close buttons.
</Aside>

On the board's change list (digits <kbd>5</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+?</kbd>), there's a toggle to show/hide the docked AI chat. Click the toggle icon (eye/crossed-eye) to open or close the chat. This also affects rail behavior: when the chat is closed, the rail hides it. When open, it opens/toggles the chat panel.
